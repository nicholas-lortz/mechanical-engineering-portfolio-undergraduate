# Project Requirements

## Design Statement

Design a centrifugal water pump to operate at the following conditions:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Rotating speed | 2500 rpm |
| Flow rate | 1500 gpm |
| Discharge head | 500 ft |

The pump operates at sea level and STP conditions.

## Design Process Guidelines

1. Select an appropriate impeller and determine the pump inlet and outlet configurations. Design the blade profile and determine the blade and flow velocities and angles, and blade dimensions.
2. Design a diffuser and volute for the pump impeller.
3. Determine the shaft power delivered by the pump.

## Deliverable

A final project report showing the design steps, calculations, sketches, reference tables, graphs, and data used in the design process.
